What is IEEE?

The Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) is the world’s largest technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity. With over 430,000 members across more than 160 countries, IEEE brings together engineers, scientists, and students to share knowledge, build technical standards, and foster innovation.

IEEE MUST SB

Founded in 2011, the IEEE MUST SB is a space for students who want more than just lectures; they want experience. We’re a student-run community that organizes hands-on training, speaker sessions, technical projects, and recruitment events that help you learn, grow, and lead.

We work across multiple committees; from coding and medical innovation to branding and social outreach, all led by students who are passionate about technology and teamwork.

Our Vision

To empower and inspire the next generation of engineers and leaders in technology, fostering innovation, creativity, and collaboration within the IEEE community at Misr University for Science and Technology.

Our Mission

To provide students with the tools, resources, and opportunities to thrive in their academic and professional journey, through hands-on experiences, leadership development, and community-driven projects that advance knowledge and make a lasting impact on society.

IEEE EMBS MUST SB Chapter

Our Vision

We strive to be a leading student community in the field of biomedical sciences, one that inspires innovation, promotes interdisciplinary collaboration, and fosters a culture of research and knowledge sharing. Through our activities, we aim to contribute meaningfully to the advancement of biomedical engineering both within our university and beyond.

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring together students from different backgrounds, encouraging collaboration and collective growth. We aim to support students’ personal and professional development by providing a strong link between academic life and career readiness. Through our initiatives, we work to strengthen the research capabilities of future biomedical engineers and empower them to make a lasting impact in the field.
